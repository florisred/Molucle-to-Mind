## Treatment of MS
### Therapeutic approaches
- Acute symptom management
- DMTs -> *disease modifying therapies*
- Symptomatic treatment
	- Medication, (cognitive) rehabilitation (only treats symptoms, not underlying disease)
### DMT possibilities
- A lot has been discovered the last 25 years
- After getting the diagnosis *RRMS or CIS*, few options
	1. No treatment
	2. First-line DMT
	3. Second line DMT
- After this yearly check up
	- Is the therapy effective? -> no, then we scale up treatment (could increase side effects)
### What do DMTs do? (names unimportant, learn mechanism behind it)
- Interferon injections
	- Modulate cytokines -> could reduce inflammation in CNS (we do not know this for sure)
- Teriflunomide and dimethylfumarate (oral intake)
	- inhibits active lymphocytes -> makes sense that it would reduce inflammation in CNS
- Natalizumab
	- Binds to very specific receptors on BBB, prevents t-cells from entering
	- Very nifty, prevents inflammation very effectively
- Fingolimod / siponimod / ponesimod / ozanimod
	- S1P modulators
	- Prevents lymphocytes from exiting lymph nodes -> prevents relapses
- Ocrelizumab / rituximab / ofatumumab
	- B-cel therapy
	- Destroys all B-cells -> prevent making antibodies -> prevents *CNS* inflammation
- Cladribine / alemtuzumab / stemcell therapy
	- Chemotherapy, you eliminate all immune system -> regrows healthily
- **Main side effects: more infections** 
- **However, you prevent relapses and delay progressive worsening**
### Side effects / effectivity risk
- The more effective the medicine, the higher the side effects will be
- Lower therapies (auto-injections) like interferon
	- Skin effects, hardened skin spots
	- 40% have flue-like symptoms
- Teriflunomide
	- Can cause hair loss & hypertension
- Ocrelizumab
	- Severe infections, pneumonias (you have no means of fighting infections)
- Fingolimod
	- Cardiac arrythmias
- Natalizumab 
	- PML -> massive inflammation of the brain
- alemtuzumab -> one of the most effective therapies
	- Cebereal infarctions / hemmorages
	- Auto-immune diseases
