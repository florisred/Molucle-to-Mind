## Normal brain white and gray matter
### First little overview of the normal brain, a lot of this we know
- Where cerebrum, cerebellum, and brainstem are 
- Two hemispheres connected by corpus callosum
- Outside, gray or pink substance, is the gray matter -> with cell bodies
- White matter, inside, contains nerve fibers -> communication system
- Brain is suspended in *cerebrospinal fluid*
- Axons covered in *myelin* -> insulates axons and makes the conduction [[Myelination|saltatory]]
