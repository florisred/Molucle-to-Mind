## research proposal
- Outlines the scientific rationale & research question & materials and methods
- Also make a rough outline of steps to take (timeline) of your work
- Clinical? --> you have to get it approved by an ethics board
	- Q4 2024
- Onstage application
## Go-NoGo evaluation
- discuss weather or not you are likely to finish and succeed in the internship
	- In practice its more about the rest of your team
- you can stop after this (6 weeks after start)
## Internship Report
- Deadline: 20 days after internship end-date
- either at home or at lab
- leverage overlap with academic writing course
- Manuscript styel report according to the guidelines of an existing neuroscientific journal --> 
	- less is more
- choose an appropriate journal style
## Presentation
- how you present & discussion of work is what you are evaluated on
- prior to report deadline --> gives you some time to integrate feedback into your report
	- Not mandatory, but advisable
## Evaluation
- Along the learning goals
	- formulate research plan --> internship application form, pass/fail, internship coordinator
	- Formulate hypothesis, designing experment, collect data, intepret data, write discussion
		- Internship report )50% of grade
	- Communicating results -> presentation, 25% grade
	- Work on your general academic competencies -> project execution, 25% grade, daily supervisor
# How to actually get an internship
- You are not alone in applying --> many students are looking
- So how do you make yourself stand out?
## Email enquiry
- one of the most important things 
- If your email is confusing & unclear, the chance that people will read it is actually really low
- 8-10 sentences or less
- think about who has time to reply to your email
1-2 sentences to all these points
1. why are you writing? and for waht degree
2. demonstrate motivation
3. statement about intention of internship -> what do you hoope to do
4. demonstrate why you would be suitable
5. reinforce expectation of reply -- give a suggestion for further communication
	- mention CV attachment
	- offer to drop by 
	- offer to communicate further with a PhD or Post-doc in their group
## How to prepare yourself for an interview?
- Pick papers performed in this lab, rather than through collaboration
- focus on reading their discussion sections first
- check out the lab website or network with pat interns
	- Scope out the department / lab hierarchy
	- which research projects are happening in parallel
	- which post-doc or PhD seems to ready for a student?
- Read up about the techniques used int he lab
- prepare a few questions
