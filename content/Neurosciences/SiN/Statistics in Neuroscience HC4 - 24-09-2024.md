## Rules contrasts
- Rule 1: choose sensible comparisons
	- You want to compare two chunks of variation, choose wisely
- Rule 2: Groups coded with positive weights will be compared against groups coded with negative weights
- Rule 3: The sum of weights for ac comparison should be zero
- Rule 4: IF a group is not in a comparison, assign it with a weight of 0
- Rule 5: for a given contrast the weights assigned to the groups should in one chunk of the variation should be equal tot he number of groups in the opposite chunk of variation
