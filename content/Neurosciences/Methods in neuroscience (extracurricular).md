## Why do we use EEG?
- Invented exactly 100 years ago, remained very similar over time
- Can be used to study basic aspects of the brain & information processing
	- If we see a face, many different brain areas fire synchronously
- Inexpensive
- However, after 100 years of use, EEG is behind on other physiological measurements
	- Premature babies are never hooked up to EEG, while it would be easy
- Advantages:
	- Direct reflection of activity -> not like MRI
	- Greater specificity -> one fMRI vocel generates functionally distinct oscillations
	- High temporal resolution
	- More portable and non-invasive, so more available and inexpensive
